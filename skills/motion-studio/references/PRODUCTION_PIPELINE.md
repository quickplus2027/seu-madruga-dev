# Video Pipeline: From Brief to Evidence

## Brief card
`Audience | objective | format | aspect | duration | fps | platform | CTA | brand | language | assets | audio | legal restrictions | references | deadline`.

Do not invent unknown parameters without flagging creative defaults. Confirm only missing information that materially changes the execution.

## Frame map
| Scene | In/Out | Goal | Visual | Motion language | Audio | Proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | frames 0..n | attract attention | signature idea | deliberate | sound cue | still + excerpt |
| Context | next | explain problem | typography/product | controlled | voice/beat | still |
| Payoff | next | reveal value | hero moment | contrast | climax | excerpt |
| CTA | ending | next action | brand/copy | readable hold | resolution | still |

Compute frame counts from declared FPS and duration; check total duration consistency. Plan visual holds for reading and caption accessibility.

## Rendering ladder
1. Static storyboard or reference frames.
2. Single scene preview (short range).
3. Low-cost full draft where appropriate.
4. Final encode matching required aspect, codec and deliverable.
5. Visual and audio QA of final export.

Do not assert preview or successful render from source code alone.

## QA matrix
- **Layout:** safe areas, margins, clipping, font fallback, contrast.
- **Motion:** continuity, no unexpected jump, physical rhythm and pacing.
- **Typography:** correct copy, visible duration, accents, kerning and line breaks.
- **Audio:** narration/music levels, cues and alignment.
- **Media:** licenses, crop, resolution, loaded fonts, correct assets.
- **Export:** fps, aspect, codec, audio track, expected length, target platform.
- **Performance:** render errors/time, memory limitations, non-deterministic output.

## Deliverable report
`Concept chosen | Engine & version | Approved assets | Scenes done | Evidence paths | Tests | Risks | Remaining work | Measured tokens/render time (if available)`.

For critical defects, attach exact frame/scene rather than “looks wrong”. Never mix up “source compiles”, “still rendered”, “draft rendered” and “final export inspected”.
